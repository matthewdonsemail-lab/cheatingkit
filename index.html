Reverse engineer anything.

cheatingkit is a small service for taking a thing apart, changing its shape, and running it somewhere else. Plain words, no jargon wall.

What it is

Most tools make the simple part complicated. This one does the opposite. You bring the thing — a game, a community, a system — and the kit gives you the pieces, what each piece does, and what it can become somewhere else.

  • Take a community apartGames, communities, systems — see the pieces and what each one does.
  • Change the modalityMove a game into a different form without losing what made it work.
  • Carry it to a custom OSBring the result onto the system you actually run.
  • Say it plainlyHard ideas get short sentences. No jargon wall.
  • Start with the thingPoint at what you already have, not at the tooling.
  • Keep it runnableEvery answer ends somewhere you can actually take it.
